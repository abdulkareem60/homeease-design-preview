Logo identity · concept stage · not final artwork

HomeEaseEssentials logo concepts

Three directions for review. Each is shown in black and white first, then reversed, then in a proposed colour, then at real sizes in a website header, mobile header, social avatar and favicon.

All lettering and marks are custom vector paths drawn for this project (not a font, not an image). Colours are screen colours for discussion only. No existing logo was supplied, so none was reviewed. A reasonable reference check was done, but uniqueness and trademark availability are not guaranteed and need a professional search before registration.

Concept 1 · Typographic wordmark

Shelf wordmark

Custom monoline lettering in which every E is drawn as three equal shelves, so the name itself looks tidy and in order. “HomeEase” is set heavier and “Essentials” lighter, which separates the two halves of the long name without adding a space or changing the spelling.

1 · Black and white

2 · Proposed colour: Pine and linen

  • Primary#1F4D45RGB 31 77 69
  • Background#F4EFE6RGB 244 239 230

3 · In use (actual sizes)

Limitations

  • Long single line: about 12:1 width to height. In a 390px mobile header it must sit at about 20px tall, where “Essentials” is the weakest part.
  • Needs a separate compact mark for favicons and avatars. The “HE” tile reads as initials but is less ownable than a symbol.
  • The shelf detail of the E is subtle below about 24px height.

Concept 2 · Symbol with wordmark

Organiser symbol

A drawer organiser seen from above: two small compartments over a wide one, with one compartment filled. It says “a place for everything”, which is the practical promise of the range, and becomes a strong favicon and app icon on its own.

1 · Black and white

2 · Proposed colour: Charcoal and saffron

  • Primary#1E2A32RGB 30 42 50
  • Accent#DE9B2FRGB 222 155 47
  • Background#F6F2EARGB 246 242 234

3 · In use (actual sizes)

Limitations

  • Reference check: an earlier draft (tall left compartment, split right) recalled Trello’s two-column icon, so it was redrawn. Grid and compartment marks are common; a professional trademark search is still needed.
  • The filled compartment needs a clear colour contrast; in one-colour print it becomes solid and still reads.
  • The single-weight wordmark is long; the symbol carries recognition at small sizes.

Concept 3 · Compact monogram identity

Shelf H monogram

An H whose crossbar is a low shelf with one item resting on it: home, organised, with the essential in place. The badge pairs with a stacked “HomeEase / Essentials”, which keeps the long name compact for headers, labels and stickers.

1 · Black and white

2 · Proposed colour: Indigo and saffron

  • Primary#2B3A67RGB 43 58 103
  • Accent#E4A537RGB 228 165 55
  • Background#F5F3EERGB 245 243 238

3 · In use (actual sizes)

Limitations

  • The item (dot) is about 2.4px at 16px favicon size; it stays visible but the shelf joke only reads from 24px up.
  • H monograms are widespread; the shelf-and-item detail is what makes it ownable, so it must not be simplified away.
  • The stacked lockup is two lines, so the header logo is taller than a single-line wordmark.
